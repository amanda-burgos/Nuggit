# What's new in Nuggit

What's new, what got fixed and what changed, by date and in plain words. You can see it in Nuggit with the 📣 What's new button.

Format: `## [Title] - YYYY-MM-DD` (or `## [1.0.0] - YYYY-MM-DD` for a version), and below it `### New`, `### Changed` or `### Fixed` with one `- ` line per change. Newest goes on top. This is the English copy of CHANGELOG.md: same entries, same order, same dates.

## [Windows and Linux] - 2026-11-04

### Changed

- **Shortcuts for each system:** on Windows and Linux, shortcuts show up as "Ctrl+1", "Ctrl+N" or "Ctrl+Shift+Space" instead of ⌘, in the left sidebar, in notes, in Settings and in Help. The Dip shortcut works there now too.
- **The system tray:** on Windows and Linux, Nuggit's icons live in the system tray (on Windows, bottom right, next to the clock), and Settings, Help and the tour say so. On Mac everything stays the same.

## [New version notice] - 2026-11-04

### New

- **Is there a new version? 🎁:** when a newer version of Nuggit comes out, you get a notice with a **Download** button that opens the page to get it. Download it and replace the app: your tasks, notes and settings stay safe. If it's not a good time, hit "Not now" (Phase 15).

### Changed

- The **Question** tab is now called **QOTD** (question of the day).
- **QOTD your way:** the first time, you pick what you want your questions to be about (at least 1). The random question is now just another topic: if you don't want it, turn it off in ✏️ My topics.
- **A prettier Music tab 🎵:** big album art, a background that takes on the song's colors, little bars that dance while it plays, and your playlists always in view as cards you can play with one click.

## [Español o English] - 2026-11-04

### New

- **Nuggit in English 🇺🇸:** the first time you open Nuggit, before the tour, you pick the language: Español or English. It changes everything: tabs, widgets, Settings, Help, the tour, the Question of the day, the menu bar menu and notifications. Dip and the other AI helpers answer in that language too. Change it whenever you want in Settings → General (Phase 14).

## [Your font] - 2026-11-04

### New

- **Pick your font 🔤:** in Settings → Appearance → Font you choose Nuggit's font from several built-in ones (rounded, bookish, handwritten, dyslexia-friendly, easier to read…) or any font you have on your Mac. Titles can use a different one (Phase 13).
- **Text size:** smaller or bigger (85% to 130%), and if you want, it can adjust itself to the window size.
- **Font per widget:** each widget can have its own from its ⚙︎, plus its own **text size** (up to 250%), perfect for a big clock.

### Changed

- **The clock grows bigger:** in a big box, the time now looks way bigger.
- **Everything fits the window:** with "Fit everything to the window size" it's not just the text anymore: the sidebar and the widgets also grow or shrink, like zooming.
- **The Nuggit logo** (top left) takes you Home.
- **What's new in English:** if you use Nuggit in English, the news show up in English too.

### Fixed

- In the tour, the "Pop them out" step pointed at an empty box; now the widget's buttons show.

## [Reminders] - 2026-11-02

### New

- **Reminders ⏰:** tell Nuggit what to remind you about and when (or tap "In 15 min", "In 1 hour", "Tomorrow 9:00"). Once or repeating: every day, some days or every X days. It lets you know with a Mac notification even if you close the window, as long as Nuggit is open in the menu bar. Not a good time? Snooze it 10 min or 1 hour. New tab and widget (Phase 12).

## [Question of the day] - 2026-10-31

### New

- **Question of the day 💭:** a question for you every day: fun, get-to-know-yourself, thought-provoking, "would you rather?" or "imagine". Not feeling it? 🔀 Another one. Write your answer (it saves itself), look at your answers from other days in the **Question** tab, or tap 💬 **Chat with Dip** to talk about it. It's also a widget (Phase 11).
- **Your topics in the Question of the day:** in ✏️ My topics you pick what you like (math, history, science, space, music, video games…) and each topic gets its own little card with its own question of the day. Answer whichever ones you want. Your topic isn't there? Type it ("K-pop", "🧪 Chemistry") and it'll get its own questions too (Phase 11).

### Changed

- **Bye, Lockdown:** since it didn't really lock the Mac (you could get out with ⌘Tab), we took it out for now. Focus stays the same.

## [Help, tour and your own color] - 2026-10-30

### New

- **What's new 📣:** a button in the left sidebar with every change to Nuggit and its date. A little number tells you how many updates you haven't seen.
- **Help ❓:** a new tab with how to use everything, where to customize it (with a button straight to that setting), FAQs and keyboard shortcuts, with search (Phase 10).
- **Welcome tour 👋:** the first time you open Nuggit, little bubbles show you the board, how to pop out widgets, the menu bar, Dip and Settings. You can skip it and replay it from Help (Phase 10).
- **What's new ✨:** after each update, a little window tells you what's new. Turn it off in Settings → General (Phase 10).
- **Ask Dip how to use Nuggit:** "how do I set a background?" and it answers with the same text as Help (Phase 10).
- **Your own color 🎨:** in Theme, "My color" paints the whole app in the color you pick (Phase 9).
- **Transparent background:** the Home background now has transparency and can be used on every tab (Phase 9).

### Changed

- **New icon:** Dip's bubble with its lightning bolt, on navy blue (Phase 9).

## [Make it yours and music everywhere] - 2026-10-23

### New

- **Make it yours 🎨:** in Settings → Appearance you pick the accent color, the board background (a color or your own photo) and how widgets look: their background, transparency, corners and shadow. Each widget can also have its own color or photo from its ⚙︎, and the text is always readable (Phase 9).
- **Image widget 🖼️:** add one photo or several that rotate on their own, to decorate your desktop (Phase 9).
- **Music anytime 🎵:** Music widget and tab with album art, ⏮ ⏯ ⏭, volume and your saved playlists (paste the link once and it shows up with its name and image). Plus a mini player from its own icon in the menu bar (Phase 8.8).

### Changed

- Smooth animations when switching tabs, when widgets appear and on buttons (respecting the Mac's "Reduce motion").

## [Calendar and music in Focus] - 2026-10-21

### New

- **Music in Focus 🎵:** paste your Spotify playlist link in Settings → Music and it plays on its own when you start focusing; it pauses on breaks (or plays a different playlist) and when you're done. Skip songs or pause from Focus and Lockdown. It uses the Spotify app, no login or Premium needed (Phase 8.7).
- **Calendar 📅** (tab ⌘0 and widget): your tasks with dates, your dailies and your Mac's events, by day. You can add a task to a day right from there (Phase 8.5).
- **Your tasks in the Mac Calendar:** connect both ways (Nuggit saves your tasks and dailies in a "Nuggit" calendar, and if you move a task to another day there, it moves here too), with **automatic or manual** sync, however you like. You can also subscribe (read-only) or export an .ics for Google Calendar (Phase 8.5).

### Changed

- Today is highlighted in the calendar.

## [Voice and calls with Dip] - 2026-10-19

### New

- **Call Dip 📞:** talk to Dip hands-free. It listens, notices when you're done, answers out loud, and if you talk while it's talking, it stops. You can ask it things like "add an English homework task for tomorrow" and confirm with a "yes". Your voice is understood on your Mac with whisper (Phase 8.3).
- **Settings in tabs** with search: General, Appearance, Dip, AI, Voice, Menu bar and About. New: open Nuggit when your Mac starts (Phase 8.3).
- **Pick Dip's voice** from a list with ▶ to hear each one (Phase 8.3).
- **Voice:** tap 🎤 to talk to Dip (or dictate your Brain Dump) with Mac dictation, and turn on 🔊 so Dip answers out loud. Pick its voice and speed in Settings → Voice (Phase 8).

### Fixed

- **Dip doesn't sound so robotic anymore:** it used to pick an old synthetic voice (Eddy). Now it uses the best Spanish voice installed, and in Settings → Voice you choose from the system voices, marked ⭐ Premium and ✨ Enhanced, with speed, pitch and a button to try it. If your Mac doesn't have good voices, it explains how to download them for free.

## [Notes, Brain Dump, Focus and Lockdown] - 2026-10-18

### New

- **Focus:** 25/5, 15/5 or whatever-you-want timer, with the task you're working on. You can pause, cancel and change it anytime, and it keeps running even if you close the window (Phase 7).
- **Lockdown:** a focus screen with just your task, the clock and the next step; it hides widgets and you can leave whenever you want with Esc (Phase 7).
- **Quick notes:** **Notes** tab and widget. A new note is one click ("+ Note") or **⌘N**. They save themselves as you type (Phase 6).
- **Brain Dump:** dump everything in your head into one box. Dip splits it into tasks and notes so you can review them before saving; without AI, it's saved as a note (Phase 6).

## [Dip's agents] - 2026-10-17

### New

- **Dip's agents:** each part of Dip (chat, actions, Tenderizer, Dailies, Brain Dump) is an agent with its own AI, which you can choose with a `.env` file. If an answer comes out wrong, Dip gets the error and fixes it on its own. Settings shows what each one uses (Phase 5.7).

## [Dailies, pins and "What do I do now?"] - 2026-10-16

### New

- **Dailies:** routines that repeat and show up as today's tasks, with a 🔥 streak and "Plan with Dip" (Phase 5.5).
- **Real pins:** a pinned widget no longer moves or gets edited. New **Pin on top**, **Pin on back** and the **quick pickup** icon in the menu bar (Phase 5.3).
- **"📌 Pin on top" button** in plain sight on every widget in the quick pickup (🧩), to turn it on or off with one click.
- **Back widgets within reach:** the ones with **Pin on back** don't get clicks, so in the quick pickup they now have **✏️ Edit** (brings it to the front for a moment so you can move or change it, and it goes back when you hit "Done" or click somewhere else) and **Remove from back**. You never have to delete them anymore.
- **"What do I do now?"** tells you just one thing to do, connected to the menu bar and to "I'm done" (Phase 5).
- **Now** and **Tenders** tabs in the control center.

### Changed

- **Pin on top follows you:** an "always on top" widget is now on every desktop (Spaces) and on top of full-screen apps, without turning anything else on. You can turn it off in ⚙︎ → "On all desktops".

### Fixed

- "Pin on top" didn't follow you when switching desktops: now it's applied again when the window shows up.
